Common questions

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account that made the purchase. Restoring is free and never charges you again.

How do I change my ad or privacy choices?

If your region requires it, Settings shows a Privacy choices row. Tap it to review or change your consent. On iPhone you can also change tracking permission for Ruach in the iOS Settings app.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy, so progress does not move automatically to a new phone.

Are the verses accurate?

Verses are taken by script from public-domain or openly licensed translations and checked against their sources. The full list of translations and licence notices is under Licenses in the game's Settings. If you think a verse is wrong, tell us the language, the verse reference and what you see.

What ages is the game for?

Ruach is for ages 13 and over.

I found a bug.

Please email us with the details above. The game keeps a small log on your device that you can send from Settings when something goes wrong.
