Privacy policy

Ruach keeps your game on your device. We have no accounts and we do not collect your personal information ourselves. Ads are the one place where data is handled by others, and this page explains how.

Last updated 18 November 2026. This policy covers the Ruach: Bible Car Racing Game app and this website.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, upgrades, best times, Daily Run history, verses you have seen) and your settings (language, theme, controls, sound, reminders) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

The game also keeps a small log of errors on your device. It is shared only if you choose to email it to us from Settings.

No accounts, no tracking by us

You do not sign up or log in, and we run no analytics or crash-reporting service. We do not know who you are or how you play.

Ads

Ruach is free because it shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive that information.

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Privacy choices in the game's Settings.
  • iPhone. The game asks permission before tracking, as Apple requires. You can say no and still play everything. You can change it later in the iOS Settings app.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings.
  • More. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

You can remove banner and between-race ads with a one-time purchase. Optional ads that you choose to watch for a small in-game bonus remain available.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The game reads from the store whether you own the purchase, so it can unlock your purchase again on a new device when you choose Restore purchase.

Reminders

If you turn on the Daily Run reminder, the game asks for notification permission and schedules a reminder on your device. It is local only and you can turn it off at any time.

Our website and small settings file

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, it may keep standard server logs that include IP addresses. The game may also download a small settings file from this website, which can switch a feature off without an app update; that request is a normal web request and carries no information from your game.

Children

Ruach is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google or Apple, use their privacy tools. If you have questions, or believe we hold information about you, write to us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If the game's use of data changes, for example when new features are added, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
