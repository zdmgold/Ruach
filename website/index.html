Ruach: Bible Car Racing Game

Race chariots and modern cars across 16 tracks set in the lands of the Bible, and read a verse of scripture at each step of the journey.

Store links will appear here when the game is released.

What you play

16 tracks in four sets

Start on the banks of the Nile, cross the Red Sea and the Jordan, climb to Jerusalem, travel the Damascus Road and the Appian Way, then take modern cars through four original landscapes.

Chariots and cars that drive differently

Heavy chariots turn wide and shrug off knocks. Modern cars grip and drift. Every vehicle has its own handling.

Seven ways to race

A Journey through all 16 tracks, Quick Race, Time Trial against your own best lap, a Daily Run that is the same for everyone, a Weekly Circuit, an Escape Run, and Elimination.

Upgrades you earn by playing

Six upgrades drawn from Isaiah 11:2: wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge and the fear of the LORD. They are earned on the track and are never for sale.

A verse for each moment

Short verse cards appear before and after races, never during one. Verses come from public-domain and openly licensed translations, in 11 languages.

Good to know

  • Free to play. The game shows ads, and a one-time purchase removes the banner and between-race ads.
  • Single player. No account, no sign-up and no online racing.
  • For ages 13 and over.
  • Your progress is stored on your device only.
